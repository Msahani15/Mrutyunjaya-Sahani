About

Academic profile & research background

A short introduction to my research journey, academic training, and the people who shaped it.

Dr. Mrutyunjaya Sahani, Ph.D.
Department of Electrical & Computer Engineering, NUS

I am a Senior Postdoctoral Research Fellow in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the National University of Singapore, working under Prof. Sanjib Kumar Panda, Director of the Power and Energy Section. My current research spans machine-learning applications to power converter fault analysis, solar PV and BESS integration into the NUS Living Lab Microgrid, and AI-driven microgrid optimization and control system design.

Before joining NUS, I completed my Ph.D. in Electronics and Communication Engineering at SOA Deemed University, Odisha, India, under the supervision of Prof. Pradipta Kishore Dash, with a thesis on intelligent embedded systems for power system disturbance monitoring. My doctoral and early postdoctoral work focused on smart-grid fault analysis, digital signal processing, and FPGA-based embedded system design for power quality monitoring.

Over the course of my career I have held postdoctoral and research positions at IIT Bhubaneswar and the Institute of Technical Education and Research (ITER), Odisha, working on renewable energy forecasting, smart meter design, and standalone embedded systems for online smart-grid disturbance monitoring — funded by bodies including the Science and Engineering Research Board (SERB), Government of India, the Energy Market Authority (EMA) of Singapore, and Keppel Offshore & Marine.

My work sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence, signal processing, and power & energy systems, with a consistent focus on translating advanced analytics into practical, deployable solutions for smart grids, renewable integration, and sustainable energy management.

Research

Research areas & interests

My research sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence, signal processing and electrical power & energy systems — grounded in the areas below, drawn from my publication record and funded research projects.

Artificial Intelligence / Machine Learning

Neural networks, deep learning, broad learning systems (RVFLN) and data analytics applied to power system and biomedical signal problems.

Power Systems & Smart Grid

AI-driven corrective contingency analysis, smart grid disturbance monitoring, and Grid-forming control for renewable-rich power systems.

Power Quality Analysis

Variational mode decomposition, Hilbert-Huang transform, and online sequential learning machines for power quality event recognition.

Power Electronics

MPPT control, multilevel inverter topologies, and fault-tolerant switched-capacitor converters for PV and DC microgrid systems.

Embedded Systems & FPGA

FPGA-based online monitoring, CAN/AIoT communication protocols, and standalone intelligent embedded systems for grid disturbance detection.

Fault Diagnosis & Protection

Fault location estimation on compensated transmission lines, islanding detection, and protection schemes for PV-wind DC microgrids.

Renewable Energy Integration

Solar PV and wind power forecasting, and integration of PV/BESS into microgrids and living-lab testbeds at NUS.

Energy Management & Battery Systems

Battery state-of-health (SOH) and remaining-useful-life (RUL) estimation for transportation electrification systems.

Biomedical Signal Analysis

Epileptic seizure recognition and schizophrenia diagnosis from EEG signals using deep autoencoders and kernel-based classifiers.
